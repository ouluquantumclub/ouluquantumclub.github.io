ABOUT

A local community with an international outlook.

The club is a place to discuss quantum-related topics, share news, exchange ideas and stay connected with developments in quantum information and computation.

You do not need to be an expert. Whether you are taking your first steps in quantum computing or already working in the field, you are welcome to participate.
